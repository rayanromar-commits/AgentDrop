I gave my brother's girlfriend a surprise when she wouldn't stop stealing my dirty underwear
subreddit: pettyrevenge
When I (F31) was 15, my brother's girlfriend (21 at the time) would steal my dirty underwear and wear them — unwashed. My clothes had been disappearing for a while: shirts, pants, bras, then underwear. I'd already asked her to stop taking my clothes. It got confirmed when I spotted a pair I'd tossed in the laundry an hour earlier — on her, when she bent over and her pants slipped.

Growing up, I did everyone's laundry, so I knew my own underwear on sight. I didn't want to accuse her outright in case I was wrong, so I tested it. I photographed the next pair I took off, left it on top of the laundry pile, showered, and came back an hour later. Gone. When she and my brother got back from the store, she bent down for a grocery bag — and there was my "test" pair.

I asked if she was wearing my underwear. She denied it, then claimed I must have mixed them into her clean clothes. I told her they hadn't been washed — I'd just taken them off — and showed her the photo. She ran off crying. After that I started keeping dirty laundry in my room… and my clothes started vanishing again. She'd been going into my room while I was at school.

I'd had enough. So, my confession: the next time I was hanging laundry, I noticed the cactus out back by the clothesline. I took that pair I'd confronted her about — I couldn't bring myself to wear them again anyway — held them taut, rubbed them on the cactus, then left them in a "dirty" spot in my room and went to school.

I came home and they were gone. Later that day I saw her walking funny, unable to look at me. She never said a word, but through the wall we shared, I heard she needed help getting the pricklies off. Years later, after they broke up, my brother brought it up at a party: "Yes, I know that was you." We couldn't stop laughing.

It wasn't my finest moment. But she got the pokey.
